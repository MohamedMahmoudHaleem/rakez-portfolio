## The challenge

Ecovet distributes feed additives from European manufacturers such as BASF, Dr. Eckel, Biochem and ADDCON across Egypt. It needed a professional online presence that explains a technical product range clearly to farm owners and feed producers, and turns visits into quotation requests.

## What we built

A fast, responsive company website that presents the brand, its mission and its partners, with a product catalog organised by category so visitors can quickly find the right additive for their animals and get in touch.

## Key features

- Full-screen hero and clear brand story (about, mission, vision)
- Product catalog grouped into five categories: premixes, toxin binders, enzymes, gut health enhancers and organic acids
- Card and list views for browsing products
- Contact section with a quotation request form
- Optimised images, mobile-first layout and SEO-ready pages

## Result

Launched and live at ecovetegypt.com.
