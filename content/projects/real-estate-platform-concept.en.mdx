## The challenge

Real estate developers often have strong projects but weak digital presence. After delivery, owners and tenants rely on phone calls and paper for maintenance and permits, and management has little visibility into service quality.

## What we built

A concept showing one connected platform: a premium bilingual website that presents projects and construction progress, a residents app for daily requests, and a dashboard where each building's team manages everything by role.

## Key features

- Luxury bilingual website with project pages and construction progress
- Residents app: maintenance requests with photos and a live status timeline
- Visitor passes with QR codes and a gate scanning screen
- Role-based dashboard: head office sees all projects, building managers see their own
- Reports on request types, response times and satisfaction

## Result

This is a concept that demonstrates our approach to real estate platforms. It uses a fictional brand and placeholder content.
