## The challenge

Most training apps explain exercises with flat lists and static pictures, so it is hard to see which muscle a movement actually works or how a full program fits together.

## What we built

Holo Muscle presents the human body as an interactive hologram. Users explore the muscular system in 3D, select a muscle to scan it, and move from that muscle straight into the training that targets it, all inside one complete training system.

## Key features

- Full-body 3D hologram with front, side and back views
- Tap any muscle to scan it and set it as the training target
- Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom
- A complete training system organised around muscle groups
- Built ready for AI-powered guidance
